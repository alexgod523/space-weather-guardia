Colors show broad impact potential from current NOAA scales, not heat or temperature. Actual effects depend on event type, local conditions, equipment, and timing. Follow official bulletins and operating procedures.

R

Solar flare · radio blackout

R-scale flare effects on HF radio and some navigation signals are strongest on Earth's sunlit side. The map marks the subsolar point and current day/night boundary.

G

Geomagnetic storm

The NOAA G scale is planetary. The map highlights higher geographic latitudes as a broad exposure context for grid and navigation systems, not as a precise local forecast.

S

Solar radiation storm

The S scale describes energetic-particle conditions. Polar routes, high-altitude operations, and spacecraft may have different concerns; this view does not calculate personal dose.
